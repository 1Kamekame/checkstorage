Privacy Policy

CheckStorage does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers.

Information Collection

This app does not collect personal data or use analytics or tracking services.

Storage Information

CheckStorage accesses storage information, such as total and available storage capacity, to display it within the app and its widgets. This information is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

Presets and Settings

Presets and settings created within the app are stored locally on your device using Apple's App Groups to share data between the app and its widgets. This information is not transmitted to external servers.
